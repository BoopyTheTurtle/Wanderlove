Most important thing in the first date: to test a kind of complicity, a kind of animal thing under a conversational thing. An intuitive non-tangible thing. Conversation is superstructure - chemistry is in looks, feeling, body language. There can also be an intellectual complicity, but overall animal compatibility - emotional, physical and chemical.
