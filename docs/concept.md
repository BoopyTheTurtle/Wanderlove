# Wanderclue

A couples app that turns a city into a shared adventure. Partners complete location-based photo challenges and conversation prompts together, and every photo builds a shared visual map of the relationship.

**Core loop:** pick a trail → go to a spot → snap a couple photo → answer a prompt → unlock the next stop. Each photo lands on the shared relationship map.

## Modes

**First Dates.** Trails run 60–90 minutes through busy public spots, low-pressure by design. Challenges are icebreakers ("find the weirdest shop sign and explain why it's you") paired with conversation prompts at each stop. The trail ends at a café or bar, so the date has a natural next step, and daters can share their location with a friend for safety.

**Established Couples.** Rediscover trails run through neighborhoods the couple never visits; Memory Lane trails they build themselves from their own places — where they met, their first apartment. Challenges go deeper: recreate an old photo, answer "what did you think of me then?" New trails drop regularly to keep the mode alive.

**Couples Traveling.** Destination trails download before the trip and work offline, with themed packs for honeymoons, anniversaries, and weekend getaways. Challenges mix romance with exploration, and the trip produces an automatic album at the end.

## Competitive landscape

### Direct: scavenger-hunt companies with a date mode

- **Let's Roam — the biggest threat.** Self-guided, app-led hunts for couples: walk to real stops, solve clues, answer trivia, complete outdoor challenges. Includes Memory Lane, Guess Your Date, and Photo Booth games, plus photo challenges like recreating a couple's first date. One ticket covers both players, works across 3,000+ locations, and supports two-phone or one-device play.
- **Crazy Dash and 3Quest Challenge** — generic hunts with a date add-on. Crazy Dash's date version layers 6 extra bonding challenges onto the regular hunt and runs in 400+ US and Canadian cities. 3Quest works the same way and adds a remote guide who chats with players in-app.
- **Eventbrite "LOVE scavenger hunts"** — small operators selling ticketed, self-guided hunts themed around love languages: acts of service, gift-giving, physical touch.

### Indirect: couples connection apps

**Paired, Agapé, Flamme, Konda, Adeux** — daily questions, quizzes, games, widgets, date ideas, bucket lists, memories. Adeux goes further with shared photo albums, matched date ideas, and real-time location sharing. None get couples out into the city with location and photo challenges.

## Positioning

The hunt companies sell a generic hunt with a couples skin — one-off tickets, US-heavy, no reason to come back next week. The couples apps stay screen-based and mostly at home. Wanderclue sits between them: built for couples from the start, split by relationship stage, recurring, with the growing relationship map as the retention hook. A dedicated first-date mode looks underserved — competitors lump new and established couples together.

Let's Roam is the biggest threat; it already has the Memory Lane and photo mechanics. Differentiate on the relationship-stage modes, the ongoing map, and Europe.
