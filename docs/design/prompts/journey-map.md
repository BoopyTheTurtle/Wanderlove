# Journey map, concept

The home screen of the MVP (roadmap stage 6): an endless illustrated path that a couple's avatars walk along, one
point per finished quest. This first prompt explores the look; the final art gets cut into tiles that repeat
vertically. Style reference: `docs/design/mockups/Adventure start.png`, moved to the wine palette.

Edgar's reviews (September 30): the blend of town and farm, old and new, is right. The first image's level of detail
was good after all (the second, simpler one lost that). What threw him off were the markers: all the same size,
ignoring perspective, and too bright. They should shrink with distance and blend into the scenery.

## Prompt

A tall, portrait-format storybook illustration for a mobile app home screen, seen from a gentle high angle. A single
winding footpath climbs from the bottom of the image to the top, passing through a whimsical world where a small
idyllic town, rolling countryside, and the lively edge of a big city blend together: cobbled lanes and a little
bakery, orchards and a windmill, a riverside with a footbridge, then glowing city rooftops, trams, and a skyline in
the distance. Along the path sit six round stepping-stone markers set into the path itself, made of the same worn stone as
the path with a faint warm rim, evenly spaced along it. They follow the perspective: the nearest marker at the bottom
is largest, and each one further up the path is smaller, the farthest ones small and partly tucked behind the path's
bends. They are subtle, muted, part of the scenery, not glowing discs; each is empty and ready to be stood on. No people, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos.

Keep a rich storybook level of detail. The blend matters most: ancient and modern, farm and city, side by side.

Style: soft painterly gouache with visible paper grain, rounded simple shapes, gentle light, cosy and warm, like a
modern children's picture book. Palette built around deep wine red (#8b2e45), dusty rose (#f6dcd3), coral (#f2806a),
warm cream paper (#fbf0e2), with muted sage greens for trees and a soft dusk sky. Calm and uncluttered, so small
character avatars placed on the markers would stand out clearly.
