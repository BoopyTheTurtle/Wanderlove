# Avatar style sheet, concept

Explores the look of the avatar creator (roadmap stage 5). The chosen style then gets redrawn as layered SVG parts,
so the image model only sets the direction. Style reference: `docs/design/mockups/Avatar Man.png` and
`Avatar Woman.png`.

Edgar approved the first sheet's style on September 30 ("spot on"); it is the reference for the SVG parts.

## Prompt

A character style sheet on a plain warm cream background (#fbf0e2): eight friendly head-and-shoulders avatars in a
neat 4 by 2 grid, each inside a soft circle. They range from younger to older and from more masculine-looking to
more feminine-looking faces and builds, with varied skin tones, eye colours, hairstyles (short, curly, long, braids,
bald, grey), clothing (hoodie, knit jumper, denim jacket, striped shirt), and a few simple accessories (round
glasses, a beanie, earrings, a scarf). All share one consistent style: flat, rounded shapes, simple dot eyes, rosy
cheeks, a small warm smile, soft painterly texture, clean outlines-free forms that could be rebuilt as vector layers.
Circle backgrounds in dusty rose (#f6dcd3), coral (#f2806a), and soft wine tints (#8b2e45 at low saturation). No
text, no labels, no logos.
