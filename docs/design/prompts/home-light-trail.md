## Prompt

A modern mobile app home screen mockup, portrait, for a cute couples' walking-adventure app called Wanderlove. Flat,
minimal, soft vector illustration with gentle gradients and rounded shapes, far less detail than a storybook: a few
simple rolling hills, a handful of round trees, and a soft silhouette of a town and city skyline in the distance.

One smooth path winds from the bottom of the screen up into the distance. There are no circles, markers, dots or
stepping stones on it. Ahead of the couple, the path glows: a soft trail of warm coral and gold light runs up along it
and fades into the horizon, like the way forward is lighting up. Behind them the path is plain and muted.

Near the bottom third, two small, cute, chibi-style avatars of a couple stand together on the path, holding hands.

The background is a smooth gradient from deep wine red (#8b2e45) at the top to coral (#f2806a) at the bottom, with
dusty rose (#f6dcd3) and cream (#fbf0e2) highlights. Minimal UI on top: a slim status bar, a small heart logo at the
top left and a bell icon at the top right, one frosted-glass rounded card near the top showing three small stats with
icons, and a rounded bottom navigation bar with three icons. Clean, airy, lots of breathing room. Keep text to a minimum
and legible.
