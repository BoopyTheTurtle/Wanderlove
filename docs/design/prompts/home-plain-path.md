# Home screen, plain path

A second look at the Home journey (roadmap stage 6). Edgar's review (October 2): less detail, more modern, still cute.
No circles or markers on the path; this variant lets the path itself carry the journey.

## Prompt

A modern mobile app home screen mockup, portrait, for a cute couples' walking-adventure app called Wanderlove. Clean,
minimal flat illustration with soft grain and rounded shapes, in the style of a modern lifestyle app: very little
detail, large calm areas of colour.

A single soft ribbon-like footpath in cream (#fbf0e2) curves in a graceful S-shape from the bottom of the screen up to
the horizon, narrowing with perspective. Nothing sits on the path: no circles, dots, markers or stones. Only a few
simple shapes frame it: two or three rounded hills, a few lollipop trees, a tiny windmill and a faint city skyline far
away under a soft sun.

Two small, cute, rounded avatars of a couple walk side by side on the path in the lower third, with a gentle shadow.

Background gradient from deep wine red (#8b2e45) at the top to coral (#f2806a) at the bottom, with dusty rose
(#f6dcd3) accents. Minimal UI on top: a slim status bar, a small heart logo top left and a bell top right, a single
frosted-glass rounded card with three small stat icons and numbers, and a rounded bottom navigation bar with three
icons. Airy, modern, lots of whitespace. Keep text to a minimum and legible.
